Buyers already see the Wohnung. To understand the Kiez they still jump between Maps, Immo portals, PDFs, spreadsheets and a walk-by. Kiez puts that missing layer in one place — built from the Berlin address you already have.

Questions your buyers ask

The walk, the errands, the commute.

What does the walk around this home feel like?

Pitch the map and follow the block from the front door — Hinterhöfe, pavement, the actual street, not a walk-score.

What would life here look like for me?

Filter coffee, grocery, park, restaurants. The Kiez is different in Bergmannkiez than at Savignyplatz — show that.

Can I reach my everyday stops easily?

Tap a bakery or Bio-Markt and see walking minutes from this Wohnung, on the street network.

Would this commute work for me?

Type an office in Mitte, a school in Prenzlauer Berg, or Hauptbahnhof. Drive or walk time on real roads.

How would my usual day work here?

Coffee, errands, a park, dinner nearby — assembled from what’s actually mapped around the pin.
